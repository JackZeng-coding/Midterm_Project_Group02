## Use Cases
1. **Login & Authentication:** Users (Students, Faculty, Admins) can securely log into system and are routed to their dashboards (workareas) based on their role.
2. **Administer user accounts:** Admins can create, modify, and delete accounts
3. **Register Persons:** Admins can register persons as either students, faculty, or academic staff
4. **Manage Student Records:** Admins can update, delete, or view faculty data
5. **Manage own profile:** Admins can manage their own profile.
6. **Manage coursework:** Students can view, track, and submit assignments
7. **Register for classes:** Students can enroll or drop courses
8. **Perform Graduation Audits:** Students can track their credits and check their graduation requirements
9. **Review Transcript:** Students can view their academic history in their transcripts
